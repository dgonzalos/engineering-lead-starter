# Onboarding checklist

## Access and working agreements
- [ ] Confirm IT/MDM policy, approved development and AI tooling
- [ ] Confirm identity/SSO, Git provider, ticketing and documentation access
- [ ] Validate dev environment from official team instructions
- [ ] Confirm product, stakeholders, team responsibilities and decision rights

## First conversations
- [ ] Meet engineers, EM, Product, Design, QA and platform stakeholders
- [ ] Ask what works well and where developer friction arises
- [ ] Agree expectations for 30/60/90 days with manager

## Engineering baseline
- [ ] Map frontend applications, repos and owners
- [ ] Trace one change from ticket to production
- [ ] Review tests, pipelines, release cadence and incident response
- [ ] Identify existing documentation, standards and metrics

## Follow-through
- [ ] Share factual current-state notes for team corrections
- [ ] Agree a small, reversible improvement with stakeholders
