# First 30 days — adaptable plan

## Week 1: Listen and map
Learn people, product, expected outcomes, standards and permissions. Run the app locally.

## Week 2: Trace architecture and delivery
Follow a small feature through code review, testing, deployment and observability.

## Week 3: Validate opportunities
Present evidence-based pain points; ask engineers and product to challenge assumptions.

## Week 4: Align and execute
Ship or facilitate one low-risk improvement and propose 2–3 team-agreed priorities.

## Success criteria
- Shared, corrected map of team, product and frontend landscape
- Relationships established with primary stakeholders
- Explicit decision rights and expectations
- One valuable, scoped improvement (if appropriate)

Adjust to Indesia's priorities, not the calendar alone.
